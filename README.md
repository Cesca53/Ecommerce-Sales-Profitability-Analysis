# 🛒 E-commerce & Profitability Analysis

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing sales, revenue, costs, profit, products, categories, and overall business performance.

This project focuses on turning e-commerce data into **actionable business insights** that can support decisions around profitability, product performance, and sales strategy.

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions, including:

* What is the overall **sales and revenue performance**?
* How much **profit** is the business generating?
* Which **products and categories** contribute the most to revenue and profit?
* What is the relationship between **sales, costs, and profitability**?
* Which products or categories may require **further attention**?
* What trends and patterns can help support **better business decisions**?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** — Dashboard development, data visualization, and business analysis
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** — Measures, calculated columns, and KPI calculations
* **Excel** — Data preparation and initial analysis

## 📊 Dashboard

The interactive Power BI dashboard provides an overview of:

* Sales and revenue performance
* Costs and profitability
* Product and category performance
* Key business KPIs
* Trends and performance patterns

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis identified important patterns in **sales, revenue, costs, profitability, and product performance**.

Key findings include:

* Identified the products and categories contributing most significantly to revenue and profit.
* Compared sales performance with associated costs to evaluate profitability.
* Examined product and category performance to identify areas of strong and weak contribution.
* Analyzed business trends to understand changes in sales and profitability over time.

> **Note:** Specific KPI values and findings will be added to this section based on the final dashboard results.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business can:

* Prioritize products and categories with stronger revenue and profit contribution.
* Review products with high sales but weaker profitability to better understand their cost structure.
* Monitor costs alongside revenue rather than evaluating sales performance alone.
* Use performance trends to support inventory, pricing, and sales decisions.
* Continue monitoring product and category profitability to identify opportunities for improvement.
